# Week 4 Reflection — Plan and Tasks Documents

## What went well?

Using AI helped organize the ideas from the business case and specification into the required plan and task formats. The most useful part was giving the AI the actual templates and guides instead of asking it to create the documents from scratch. That helped keep the plan connected to the requirements and made the task list easier to follow.

## What did not go well?

I believe that everything in the process went mostly okay.

## How might you approach this type of work in the future?

I would keep the plan and tasks as separate prompting steps and review each one before moving on.
